About VeniVidiAV

A decision-support tool for exploring how agrivoltaic siting priorities reshape rankings, deployment portfolios, and spatial trade-offs.

Open the explorer

Purpose

What this tool is for

VeniVidiAV helps users compare agrivoltaic siting strategies under alternative objective priorities. Rather than producing one fixed “best” map, it makes visible how different choices change both the merit order of municipalities and the subset ultimately selected under a deployment scenario.

Map modes

How to read the explorer

Merit order shows the continuous ranking implied by the current pillar weights. Selection outcome shows which municipalities are actually selected once the deployment target, mobilization rate, feasibility multiplier, and density assumptions are applied.

Pillars

What the five pillars represent

Energy Solar productivity, grid access, and parcel structure.
Agricultural intensity Irrigation, pesticide pressure, and on-farm energy dependence.
Climate resilience Exposure to water stress, heat stress, and frost-related constraints.
Rural resilience Structural agricultural fragility and long-run decline indicators.
Nature conservation Ecological infrastructure and landscape context.

Data

Main data families used in the tool

The explorer combines municipality-level derived indicators for energy siting conditions, agricultural intensity, climate exposure, rural fragility, nature context, and feasibility constraints. Deployment outputs further rely on eligible agricultural area, PV productivity, and density assumptions.

Usage

Suggested workflow

  1. Start from a preset or rebalance the five pillar weights manually.
  2. Use Merit order to see how ranking patterns shift spatially.
  3. Use Selection outcome to see which municipalities are actually captured.
  4. Then adjust deployment assumptions to evaluate how scenario outputs and selected areas change.
