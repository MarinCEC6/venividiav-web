About VeniVidiAV

A decision-support tool for exploring how agrivoltaic siting priorities reshape rankings, deployment portfolios, and spatial trade-offs.

Open the explorer

Purpose

What this tool is for

VeniVidiAV helps users compare agrivoltaic siting strategies under alternative objective priorities. Rather than producing one fixed "best" map, it makes visible how different choices reshape both the merit order of municipalities and the subset ultimately selected under a deployment scenario.

Map modes

How to read the explorer

Merit order shows the continuous ranking implied by the current pillar weights. Selection outcome shows which municipalities are actually selected once the deployment target, mobilization rate, feasibility multiplier, and density assumptions are applied.

Scoring

Scoring and normalization

The explorer works with municipality-level indicators that are first oriented so that higher values always mean a better fit for the corresponding objective, then normalized onto a common scale before aggregation. Within each pillar, the component indicators are combined into one composite pillar score. Scenario weights then combine the five pillar scores into a merit order, while deployment settings determine which municipalities are ultimately selected. Full derived inputs, documentation, and reproducible workflow files are available on Zenodo.

  1. Raw spatial and statistical layers are harmonized to the municipality level.
  2. Indicators are oriented and normalized so that they can be compared across dimensions.
  3. Indicator scores are aggregated within each pillar to form composite pillar scores.
  4. Pillar weights define the merit order, and scenario settings define the final selected portfolio.

Pillars

What the five pillars contain

Each pillar combines several municipality-level indicators that are normalized onto a common scale before scenario weighting. The explorer does not display raw variables directly; it shows the resulting composite scores used in the ranking and deployment logic.

Energy Combines PV productivity, grid access, and parcel structure to represent expected yield and developability.
  • PVOUT
  • Distance to nearest substation
  • Average parcel size
Agricultural intensity Targets input-intensive systems where agrivoltaics may interact more strongly with farm practices and resource use.
  • Irrigation withdrawals per hectare
  • Pesticide intensity proxy
  • On-farm primary energy dependence
Climate resilience Captures climatic exposure conditions under which shading may provide stronger agronomic value.
  • Water deficit
  • Heat exposure
  • Frost exposure
Rural resilience Represents long-run agricultural fragility and structural decline in local farming systems.
  • Agricultural employment decline
  • SAU decline
  • PBS decline
Nature conservation Represents ecological infrastructure and landscape context in and around agricultural land.
  • Hedgerow density
  • Permanent grassland share
  • Forest context

Data

Main data blocks used in the explorer

The explorer is built from municipality-level derived indicators. Raw spatial layers are harmonized to a common municipal support before scoring and deployment accounting. The public release of the explorer inputs, derived indicators, scenario outputs, and workflow documentation is available on Zenodo: 10.5281/zenodo.19109614.

Indicator Source Measurement Role in the explorer
Feasibility mask
Eligibility share (phi) RPG (2022), Natura 2000, DEM slope, ADMIN-EXPRESS (2025) Share of eligible agricultural land (ratio; municipality level) Defines the feasible agricultural universe and scales land, capacity, and electricity outputs.
Energy pillar
E1 Grid proximity ODRE substations (2025) Distance to nearest substation (km; municipality centroid) Proxy for grid access and connection conditions.
E2 Solar resource Global Solar Atlas v2 PVOUT (kWh/kWp/year; municipality level) Captures expected photovoltaic productivity and is also used in electricity accounting.
E3 Parcel structure RPG (2022) Mean parcel area (ha/parcel; municipality level) Proxy for developability and layout conditions.
Agricultural intensity pillar
A1 Irrigation withdrawals BNPE (2016-2024) Agricultural withdrawals per hectare (m3/ha; municipality level) Proxy for irrigation pressure.
A2 Pesticide intensity ADONIS / Solagro IFT (2024) Treatment-frequency-type indicator (index; municipality level) Proxy for chemical-input intensity.
A3 On-farm energy dependence Agence ORE and ClimAgri-based proxies Primary energy use per agricultural area (municipality level) Proxy for farm energy dependence.
Climate resilience pillar
C1 Water-stress exposure Meteo-France daily climate series (1996-2024) Cumulative water deficit (mm; grid then municipality average) Captures conditions under which shading may relieve water stress.
C2 Heat exposure Meteo-France Tmax (1996-2024) Excess degree-days (°C-days; grid then municipality average) Captures exposure to damaging heat conditions.
C3 Frost exposure Meteo-France Tmin (1996-2024) Frost days / frost intensity (grid then municipality average) Captures frost-related constraints and possible protection value.
Rural resilience pillar
R1 Employment decline Agreste agricultural census series Long-run change in agricultural employment (%; harmonized to municipalities) Captures structural decline in the agricultural labour base.
R2 SAU decline Agreste SAU series Long-run change in utilized agricultural area (%; harmonized to municipalities) Captures contraction of the agricultural land base.
R3 PBS decline Agreste PBS series Long-run change in standard output (%; harmonized to municipalities) Captures structural decline in agricultural production capacity.
Nature conservation pillar
N1 Hedgerow density IGN BD TOPO (HAIE, 2024) Hedgerow length per agricultural area (m/ha; municipality level) Proxy for ecological infrastructure and habitat connectivity.
N2 Permanent grassland share RPG (2022) Share of permanent grassland in agricultural land (ratio or %; municipality level) Proxy for extensive grass-based systems and ecological value.
N3 Forest cover IGN MASQUE-FORET (2025) Forest share in municipality area or agricultural context (ratio or %; municipality level) Proxy for surrounding habitat context and landscape complexity.
Observed AV projects
BATAVIA project records BATAVIA inventory (2025) Project-level geocoded observations matched to municipality identifiers Used for the revealed-preference benchmark and BAU comparison logic.

Usage

Suggested workflow

  1. Start from a preset or rebalance the five pillar weights manually.
  2. Use Merit order to see how ranking patterns shift spatially.
  3. Use Selection outcome to see which municipalities are actually captured.
  4. Adjust deployment assumptions to evaluate how scenario outputs and selected areas change.
